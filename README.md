🦋 Kipepeo Market

Everything you need, all in one place.

Kipepeo Market is a modern online marketplace designed to connect customers with verified Kenyan sellers. The platform brings multiple product categories together in one convenient shopping destination, making online shopping easier, more accessible and more convenient.

🌟 Project Overview

Kipepeo Market aims to empower local businesses by helping sellers showcase their products online while giving customers a convenient way to discover and purchase everyday essentials.

🎯 Problem Statement

Many customers experience difficulties when shopping online, including:

Difficulty identifying trustworthy sellers.

Products scattered across different online platforms.

Limited convenient payment options.

Lack of confidence in online transactions.

💡 Our Solution

Kipepeo Market provides a centralized online marketplace where customers can shop from different product categories and pay using convenient payment methods.

🛍️ Product Categories

📱 Electronics

🍳 Kitchenware

👗 Apparel

🌿 Wellness

✨ Key Features

Customer registration and login.

Verified seller profiles.

Product catalogue and search.

Shopping cart.

Secure checkout.

M-Pesa STK Push integration.

Debit and credit card payments.

Order management and tracking.

Seller and administrator dashboards.

Mobile-responsive design.

💳 Payment Methods

The proposed payment options include:

M-Pesa STK Push.

Debit cards.

Credit cards.

Payment integrations will require the appropriate payment provider accounts, credentials and configuration.

🎯 Mission

To connect customers with reliable Kenyan sellers through convenient technology, accessible payments and excellent shopping experiences.

🌍 Vision

To become one of Kenya's most trusted and accessible online marketplaces.

💼 Business Model

Kipepeo Market plans to generate revenue through:

Commission on completed transactions.

Promoted product listings.

Seller subscription plans.

Business and delivery partnerships.

🛠️ Technology

The final technology stack will be documented as the application is developed. Planned components include:

Frontend: Modern responsive web interface.

Backend: Database and application services.

Payments: M-Pesa and card payment providers.

Hosting: Cloud-based deployment.
